BeyondHands-Limited

Compassionate support. Practical help. Peace of mind.

BeyondHands-Limited is a dedicated service platform (hosted at beyondhands.netlify.app) that provides calm, practical, and personal support for carers and families caring for older adults or vulnerable loved ones at home. Their goal is to help individuals stay comfortable, independent, and well-looked after in familiar surroundings while giving carers the breathing space and reassurance they need.

 Key Features & Services

Beyond Hands bridges the gap in formal support by offering tailored, dependable assistance across several core areas:

Benefits & Forms Support: Clear guidance through complex application forms (e.g., Attendance Allowance, Carer's Allowance, housing support) to help families access entitled benefits.

Care Paperwork & Organisation: Bringing order to care plans, medical notes, records, and important documents.

Calls & Follow-Ups: Handling calls to GP surgeries, social services, and other care agencies on behalf of the family.

Appointments & Referrals: Coordinating and tracking healthcare appointments and care pathways.

Home Companionship & Respite: Providing gentle company, conversation, tea preparation, and dependable breaks for family carers.

Hospital Discharge Support: Helping families understand next steps and expediting arrangements safely after discharge.

Family Support & Planning: Designing practical daily care routines and long-term support plans.

Regular Check-Ins: Offering consistent phone contact, night calls, and reassurance to reduce isolation.

 Flexible Support Packages

Beyond Hands adapts to each family's unique requirements with transparent pricing and no hidden surprises:

Hourly Support: Ideal for short calls, form filling, paperwork, or one-off tasks.

Care Relief Packages: Perfect for home support sessions or regular respite breaks.

Monthly Retainer: Designed for ongoing case management, regular follow-ups, and long-term peace of mind.
