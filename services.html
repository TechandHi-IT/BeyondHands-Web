What we offer

We provide dependable, compassionate support that helps families feel less stretched and more informed. Every service can be tailored to your situation, whether you need one-off help, a helping hand with daily tasks or ongoing support at home.

Benefits, Grants & Form Help

We help families understand what support is available and guide them through forms for benefits, financial help, local authority support and care-related applications. We make the process clearer, calmer and less intimidating.

  • Clear guidance through confusing paperwork
  • Support with applications and follow-up questions

Care Planning & Record Keeping

We help organise important documents, care notes, routines and key information so families can stay on top of care arrangements without feeling lost in the details.

  • Organised records and support plans
  • A practical overview of what matters most

Making Calls on Your Behalf

We can contact agencies, GP services, social care teams and other professionals to chase updates, ask questions and help move things forward when time is short and stress is high.

  • Someone to take the pressure off phone calls
  • Follow-through and clear communication

Appointments, Referrals & Follow-Up

We help families arrange appointments, chase referrals and keep care plans moving. This means less missed follow-up and a stronger sense of control.

  • Support keeping appointments organised
  • Gentle reminders and consistent follow-up

Hospital Discharge Guidance

After a hospital stay, many families feel unsure what comes next. We help explain the next steps, make sense of arrangements and ensure important support is put in place promptly.

  • Clear guidance after discharge
  • Help reducing confusion at a stressful time

Home Support & Companionship

We offer gentle, reassuring support in the home, including conversation, refreshments, companionship and practical help that allows carers to take a break and breathe.

  • Warm and respectful home support
  • A calming presence for both the cared-for person and the family

Regular Check-Ins & Reassurance

We can provide regular check-ins for comfort, reassurance and updates, especially when families are juggling a lot and need a dependable point of contact.

  • Ongoing contact when things feel uncertain
  • A trusted voice to turn to

Family Support & Practical Planning

We help families make sense of care needs, routines and the day-to-day support that might be needed, giving everyone a clearer plan and a stronger sense of confidence.

  • Support with care planning and direction
  • Practical help so no one feels alone in the process

A few helpful answers

If you have a question that isn’t covered here, get in touch and we’ll be happy to talk it through.

Can I ask for help with just one task?

Yes. You can get in touch about one-off help or ask about ongoing support. We’ll talk through what you need and what is available.

How do we get started?

Start with a no-pressure conversation. We’ll listen, answer your questions and discuss possible next steps together.

How much does support cost?

Costs depend on the type and amount of support. We’ll discuss a clear quote with you before you decide to go ahead.

Do you replace medical or emergency services?

No. Beyond Hands offers practical and companionship support, not medical care or emergency response. For urgent medical help, contact NHS 111 or call 999 in an emergency.

Who our support is for

We work with families caring for older adults, vulnerable loved ones and anyone needing a little extra support at home. Our care is gentle, responsive and built around real-life needs.

A

If you are feeling stretched, we can help you take a breath and create some much-needed breathing space.

B

C

People needing steady support

If you want a consistent helping hand, we can offer ongoing support that grows with your needs.
