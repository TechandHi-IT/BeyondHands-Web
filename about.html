A little about us

Care should feel like
someone’s in your corner.

Beyond Hands was built to support families when life feels busy, emotional and complicated. We believe care should feel calm, respectful and practical, whether it is for one conversation or ongoing help at home.

Book Your Consultation Explore Services

Who we are

We are a compassionate support service helping families navigate the emotional and practical challenges of caring for a loved one at home. Our work is centred on kindness, trust and real-world help that makes everyday life easier.

What matters to us

We understand that families do not always need grand solutions. Often, what matters most is someone who listens, helps with the paperwork, arranges the next step and offers reassurance when everything feels uncertain.

How we support families

Our support can include administrative help, care-related planning, check-ins, follow-ups and gentle home-based assistance. We always aim to provide a personal touch and a calm and practical approach.
