1. Who We Are and Data Controller Details

Beyond Hands Limited functions as an independent support connector linking families and older adults to external care providers, support services, and pharmacies. We provide practical non-medical home support, companionship, and administrative assistance.

For the purposes of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, Beyond Hands Limited is the Data Controller.

2. Types of Data We Collect & Scope Limitations

We collect and process personal data that is strictly necessary to connect clients to care providers and deliver administrative assistance via our website contact and booking forms. Data collection is limited to:

  • Identity and Contact Data: Full names, email addresses, telephone numbers, and basic support inquiries.
  • Scope Clarification on Sensitive Data: Detailed medical care summaries, medical care plans, and sensitive vulnerability assessments are not collected directly through website form submissions; these are gathered securely during offline intake, consultations, or authorized care coordination. Any special category data handled subsequently is processed under strict safeguards pursuant to UK GDPR (United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation) Article 9.
  • Technical and Usage Data: IP (Internet Protocol) addresses, device information, browser details, and cookie consent preferences recorded during website visits.

Note on Operational Scope: In accordance with current platform architecture, website data collection excludes shopping cart, online payment card processing, and subscription management features.

3. Data Encryption & Security Safeguards

We maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure data security:

  • 48-Hour Encryption Mandate: Client data, vulnerability records, and support logs are automatically encrypted within 48 hours to protect sensitive information from unauthorized access or potential breaches.
  • Transmission Security: All digital interactions and web form submissions are secured using HTTPS (Hypertext Transfer Protocol Secure) encryption.

4. Lawful Basis and Purposes for Processing

We process personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a Contract: To deliver agreed support plans, administrative guidance, and connection services.
  • Explicit Consent (Article 9, UK GDPR – United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation): Required for handling special category health data and third-party liaison authorisations where applicable.
  • Legitimate Interests: To operate our support services efficiently, communicate with primary carers, and support client welfare.
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with UK (United Kingdom) statutory, accounting, and tax regulations.
  • Vital Interests & Safeguarding: To protect individuals in emergency situations or where there is a risk of serious harm.

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Processors

We treat all disclosures with strict confidentiality. Personal data is never sold or rented. We share data only with trusted third parties under formal data processing agreements, including:

  • Communication Platforms: Secure email and telecommunication providers.
  • Statutory and Medical Bodies: GP (General Practitioner) surgeries, social services, care agencies, and pharmacies, strictly when authorized by signed client proxy consent.
  • Emergency Services: Where required to protect vital interests or comply with legal obligations.

6. Data Retention Schedule

Personal data and interaction records are retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill operational, legal, and regulatory duties:

  • Website Visitor & Security Logs: Retained for 12 months for security screening, compliance, and audit trails.

7. Interactive Care Planner & Cookie Policy

  • Care Planner Tool: Selections made within the interactive Care Planner checklist reside exclusively within client-side browser local storage (LocalStorage) and are not transmitted to backend servers unless explicitly submitted via an email.
  • Website Cookies: Our website uses cookies in compliance with PECR (Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations). The cookie consent banner provides distinct buttons to manage functional cookies and opt-in marketing cookies independently. Non-essential cookies are only loaded after explicit user consent.

8. Your Data Protection Rights

Under UK GDPR (United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation), data subjects possess the following rights:

  • Access to personal data held by us.
  • Correction of inaccurate or incomplete records.
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten") where applicable.
  • Restriction or objection to specific processing activities.
  • Data portability.

To exercise any of these rights, email Hello@BeyondHands.co.uk. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113.
