What happens after I send a consultation request?

We’ll use the contact details and preferences you provide to get in touch and discuss a suitable time to speak. Sending the form is a request, not a confirmed appointment.

Do I have to commit to a service?

No. The first conversation is a chance to explain what is happening, ask questions and explore possible next steps. You can decide what feels right for you afterwards.

Can I ask for help with just one task?

Yes. You can get in touch about one-off help or ask about ongoing support. We’ll talk through what you need and what may be available.

What kind of support can you provide?

Support may include help with forms and care paperwork, practical planning, appointments and referrals, hospital discharge guidance, companionship and regular check-ins. Visit our services page for more detail.

How much does support cost?

Costs depend on the type and amount of support. We’ll discuss a clear quote with you before you decide to go ahead.

What should I include in the request form?

A short overview of the support you’re looking for is enough. Please don’t include sensitive medical or financial details in the form; we can discuss any relevant personal information directly.

Can I suggest a day or time to talk?

Yes. The form lets you share a preferred day and time of day. These are preferences only; we’ll contact you to agree a suitable time.

Do you provide medical care or emergency support?

No. Beyond Hands offers practical and companionship support, not medical care or emergency response. For urgent medical help, contact NHS 111 or call 999 in an emergency.
