How We Can Help

Whether you need a single task completed or steady ongoing support, we tailor each visit to your family’s needs with compassion and care.

See our full services page

Benefits & Forms Support

We guide you through complex forms and help you access the support you may be entitled to, including Attendance Allowance, Carer's Allowance and housing support.

  • Clear guidance and form completion
  • Help reducing stress and avoiding mistakes

Care Paperwork & Organisation

We bring order to care plans, medical notes, records and important documents so nothing important is overlooked.

  • Organised paperwork and key information
  • Practical help keeping everything in one place

Calls & Follow-Ups on Your Behalf

We make calls to GP surgeries, social services, agencies and other professionals so you are not left chasing answers alone.

  • Time-saving support and follow-through
  • Help getting updates and next steps

Appointments & Referrals

We help organise appointments, chase referrals and make sure your loved one’s care pathway stays on track.

  • Reminder and coordination support
  • Less stress and fewer missed opportunities

Home Companionship & Respite

We can sit with your loved one, provide company, make tea, share conversation and give you a much-needed break.

  • Gentle companionship and reassurance
  • A chance for carers to rest

Hospital Discharge Support

We can help families understand what comes next after discharge and make sure arrangements are put in place without delay.

  • Guidance through the next steps
  • Help reducing confusion at a stressful time

Family Support & Planning

We help you think through daily care needs, routines and practical plans so everyone knows what support is available.

  • Calm, practical planning support
  • Helping families feel prepared

Regular Check-Ins

Ongoing contact can be arranged to keep you updated, reduce isolation and offer reassurance when things feel uncertain.

  • Consistent support and contact
  • A reassuring presence when you need it

About Us

Trusted support for families who want care that feels calm, personal and dependable.

Beyond Hands was created for families who want more than a rushed service. We offer thoughtful, practical support that helps people feel looked after at home while giving carers the reassurance and breathing space they need.

Why Families Choose Us

  • We understand the pressure families face as care needs grow and systems feel complicated.
  • We take the stress out of admin, paperwork and difficult phone calls.
  • Many families do not receive enough formal support, and we help fill that gap with kindness and practical help.
  • Our support is reliable, gentle and built around trust.
  • We are compassionate, respectful and focused on making life feel more manageable.
